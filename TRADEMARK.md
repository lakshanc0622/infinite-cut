# Infinite Cut name and logo

The Infinite Cut **source code** is free software under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
The **name and logo** are not part of that grant — the AGPL conveys no
trademark rights (section 7(e)), and this project follows the same split
the Rust project, Mozilla and Docker do, and the same one Concat asks for
upstream.

## The marks

- The word mark **Infinite Cut**, as the name of this video editor.
- The Infinite Cut logo and its variants, in [`assets/`](assets/).

Both are held by Infinite Minds Academy.

## Fine without asking

- **Saying what your software is.** "a plugin for Infinite Cut", "a fork of
  Infinite Cut", "opens Infinite Cut projects". Describe the relationship
  accurately and no permission is needed.
- **Redistributing unmodified builds** under the name Infinite Cut.
- **Packaging it** for a distribution from unmodified source, keeping the
  name, with patches confined to packaging and build flags.
- **Writing about it** — reviews, tutorials, screenshots, course material,
  comparisons.

## Needs a different name

If you distribute a **modified** Infinite Cut, give it your own name and
your own icon — the same courtesy this project was asked for and gave.
Concretely: rename before you ship if you change the editor's behaviour,
engine, defaults or bundled models, add or remove features, or build
something users could mistake for an official release.

Every right the AGPL grants is still yours: fork it, change it, sell it,
host it. Just call it something else, so bug reports land in the right
place.

## Not allowed

- Presenting a modified build as an official Infinite Cut release.
- Using **Infinite Cut**, or a confusingly similar name, as your product
  name, company name, app-store listing or domain in a way implying this
  project endorses or maintains it.
- Using the logo as your own product's icon.
- Registering the mark, or a confusingly similar one, anywhere.

## Concat's marks

**Concat** and its logo belong to the upstream project and are not used
here. Nothing in this repository grants any right in them; their policy is
at <https://github.com/jub0t/concat/blob/main/TRADEMARK.md>. See
[`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md).
